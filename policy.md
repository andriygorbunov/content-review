# Content Policy (v1)

A piece of content is **violating** if it matches any rule below. Otherwise **ok**.

1. **Threats / violence** — expresses intent to harm a person or group.
2. **Harassment** — targeted insults or degrading language aimed at a person.
3. **Spam / scam** — unsolicited promotion, giveaways, "click here" bait.
4. **Privacy** — shares or solicits personal identifying information (doxxing).

Notes for reviewers (and for the model prompt):
- Criticism of an *idea, product, or company* is NOT harassment.
- Quoting or reporting violating content in order to condemn it is NOT violating.
- Ambiguity resolves to **ok**: in enforcement, a false positive (wrongful
  action against a real user) costs more than a false negative.
